**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft  
**Related Documents:** PRD.md, TRD.md, APP_FLOW.md

# 1. Design Vision

Nila should feel like a premium modern AI product while maintaining a warm, calm and trustworthy feeling.

The visual direction is inspired by the quality and polish of modern AI SaaS products such as Lokam AI.

Lokam AI is a visual reference only.

Nila must have its own:

- branding
- typography
- layout
- illustrations
- icons
- animations
- copy
- visual identity

Do not copy proprietary assets or reproduce exact layouts.

## Design Principle

> Premium technology on the surface, human connection at the center.

The interface should feel:

- calm
- premium
- trustworthy
- warm
- minimal
- modern
- spacious
- emotionally positive

It should NOT feel:

- clinical
- hospital-like
- overly corporate
- childish
- overly futuristic
- crowded

---

# 2. Visual Direction

## Overall Style

Use a modern AI SaaS aesthetic:

- dark premium sections
- clean light dashboard surfaces
- large typography
- subtle gradients
- soft glow effects
- rounded cards
- generous whitespace
- subtle borders
- smooth transitions
- restrained use of glass effects
- high-quality micro-interactions

The design should prioritize readability over decoration.

---

# 3. Brand Identity

## Product Name

**Nila**

The name should be presented simply.

Avoid adding unnecessary words such as:

> Nila AI Platform

Prefer:

> Nila

---

# 4. Brand Message

Primary message:

> **Distance shouldn't mean missing the little things.**

Supporting message:

> Nila helps families stay connected through regular AI conversations with elderly parents and meaningful context for the people who care about them.

Alternative supporting copy:

> A little closer, even from miles away.

---

# 5. Typography

Use a modern sans-serif font.

Preferred direction:

- Inter
- Geist
- Manrope

Use one primary font family consistently.

## Typography hierarchy

### Display

Large landing-page headings.

Example:

> Distance shouldn't mean  
> missing the little things.

### Heading

Used for major sections.

### Subheading

Used for supporting descriptions.

### Body

Highly readable and comfortable.

### Caption

Used for metadata such as:

> Last check-in · Today, 6:32 PM

Avoid excessive font weights.

---

# 6. Color System

Use a restrained palette.

## Primary

A deep near-black / dark navy foundation for premium sections.

## Background

Use soft off-white/light neutral surfaces for the dashboard.

## Accent

Use a calm blue-violet or similarly subtle AI-oriented accent.

The accent should not dominate the interface.

## Semantic colors

### Success

Used for:

- completed calls
- positive status
- healthy system state

### Warning

Used for:

- follow-up items
- repeated observations

### Urgent

Used only for:

- urgent human-attention events

Do not use red for ordinary concerns.

---
